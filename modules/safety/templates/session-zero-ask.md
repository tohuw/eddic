# The session-zero ask

Send this privately to each player, one at a time — a direct message,
not a channel. The DM answers it too: the DM's own limits belong in the
record on the same terms as anyone else's. Re-send it unchanged
whenever the record is revisited, so answering it is never a signal
that something has come up.

Replace the bracketed bits and send the rest as written.

---

Before we start [CAMPAIGN NAME] I ask everyone the same three
questions, privately. There are no wrong answers, you don't have to
explain any of them, and "nothing comes to mind" is a complete answer
that plenty of people give.

**1. Lines.** Is there anything you don't want in this game at all —
not on screen, not off screen, not in description or backstory? Name
it as loosely or as precisely as you like.

**2. Veils.** Is there anything that can exist in the world but that
you'd rather we cut away from rather than play out? We'd pick up
afterwards, with whatever happened having happened.

**3. Sharing.** For each thing you named: is it fine for it to go on
the table's safety page, or would you rather only I knew? The page
never says who asked for anything either way — that part is not
recorded anywhere the table can reach. Choosing "only you" is for when
the entry itself would point at you.

Two more things, and then I'll leave you alone about it.

You can change any of this at any point in the campaign, by messaging
me. Adding something later is exactly as normal as adding it now, and
nothing about the change is announced.

And during play, if something needs to stop, you have four ways and
they all work the same: say "X-card" out loud, type X in
[TEXT CHANNEL], react with [REACTION] on the pinned post, or message
me. Whichever you use, we back the scene up and it goes differently. I
won't ask what it was, then or later, and neither will anyone else.
Leaving the call is fine too and needs no explanation.
