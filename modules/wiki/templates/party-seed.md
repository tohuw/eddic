# The Party

The player characters, at a glance. This page is the answer to the
question a table asks most often — "who are we again?" — and it is also
the machine-readable roster: the character pages it links to *are* the
party, so anything that needs to know which characters are played by
people reads this page rather than a flag scattered across N files.
